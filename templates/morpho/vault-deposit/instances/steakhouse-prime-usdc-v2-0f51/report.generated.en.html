The Safe first approves VaultBundlesV1 for exactly the amount you type, then calls vaultBundlesV1Deposit on it: VaultBundlesV1 pulls the tokens from the Safe and deposits them into the vault you pick, and the vault mints the shares straight to the Safe. You do not type the minimum shares: the program computes them from the vault's own preview when you build the card, less a small slippage; a higher price per share makes the transaction fail. Where deposits goAI research

Your USDC does not stay in the vault: in the same transaction the vault hands it to its liquidity adapter, which supplies it to one Morpho Blue market, USDC lent against WBTC at 86% LLTV (block 26,490,392). An allocator can point it at another market at any time. source 1 source 2 source 3 source 4

Which markets it lends toAI research

Through its one adapter the vault lends to 9 Morpho Blue USDC markets. Allocators move USDC between them within caps the curator sets per adapter, collateral and market, so the mix changes whenever they reallocate. source 1 source 2 source 3

Who can change itAI research

Three allocators can move funds between markets at any time, within the caps. The curator must wait 7 days to add an adapter or raise a cap, but can add an allocator, change the fees or set the withdrawal penalty at once. source 1 source 2 source 3

Getting the USDC backAI research

Nothing sits idle, so a withdrawal is paid from the WBTC market the adapter supplies: about 47.6 million of the vault's 114.7 million USDC could leave that way (Morpho's API, 2026-11-25). Beyond that, wait for an allocator to move USDC back, or call forceDeallocate and pay its penalty (0.001% now; the curator can raise it to 2% at once). source 1 source 2 source 3 source 4
