Submit a Morpho Vault V2 collateral cap increase (curator)AI research

morpho/vault-v2-collateral-cap-submit · kpk-usdc-prime-v2-9ee6

As curator, the Safe calls the vault's multicall with two submit calls inside: one queues raising the absolute cap of the collateral token you pick to the amount you type, the other raising its relative cap to the percentage you type. One collateral cap limits every market of that collateral together. This transaction only queues; once the vault's timelock has passed, anyone can execute it. It moves none of the Safe's assets.AI research

  • Code unchanged 8 / 8 At each signing, the program checks that every contract the card uses still runs the code it was researched with. An upgrade or a replacement is stopped. Signing does not touch vault-v2-factory, morpho-market-v1-adapter-v2-factory, kpk-usdc-prime-v2-9ee6-market-adapter; only the report compares their code. No ABI proof compares the code kpk-usdc-prime-v2-9ee6-market-adapter runs today, so the report reads it at the latest block and compares it with the current code pin.
  • Parameter checks no tests The template has no argument that takes any value: each is bound to the card or to a fixed value, so nothing needs a perturbation test and none is recorded. At each signing, verify changes every bound argument one by one and checks that each becomes a MISMATCH.
  • Two providers Every chain read asks two providers; when they disagree, there is no result. Three research reads have one source, and none of them reaches a verdict: past transactions' call traces (drpc), the search for past transactions (Alchemy or Etherscan finds them), and the addresses that ever received a vault's shares (Alchemy; each one's balance is still read through both).

This template accepts one collateral, with one submit each for the absolute and the relative cap, the shape established curators sent (21 such transactions in the recent history of 265 curator Safes). Several ids in one transaction get MISMATCH from verify.AI research
AdvicePut one id in each transaction. On MISMATCH, do not sign.AI research
The card lists every registry token as a collateral, including tokens no market of this vault uses. A cap on a collateral no market uses lets nothing in by itself: a market cap is needed too, and the adapter checks all three ids on each allocation. sourceAI research
AdvicePick the collateral of the markets you mean to open; check its address on the card.AI research
This vault holds about 31.7 million USDC. 97 addresses hold shares; the largest has 50.61% and the top 10 hold 94.31%. It held under 9 million USDC until 2026-04, then 20 million to 31.7 million from 2026-05 on.AI research
AdviceHalf the shares sit with one holder, so a cap change moves mostly that holder's funds. Set the cap by your risk framework.AI research
This vault's timelock for both increaseAbsoluteCap and increaseRelativeCap is 3 days and 1 second (timelock read on chain, 259201 seconds). For that time after it is sent, depositors can see this change, and the sentinel or curator can cancel it with revoke.AI research
AdviceIf you find a mistake within 3 days of sending, cancel it with revoke; do not wait for it to be executed.AI research
