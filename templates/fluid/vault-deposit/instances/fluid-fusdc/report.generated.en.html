Deposit into a Fluid lending pool (fToken)AI research

fluid/vault-deposit · fluid-fusdc

The Safe first gives the fToken you pick an allowance of exactly the amount you type, then calls deposit on the same fToken. Through Fluid's Liquidity contract, the fToken pulls these tokens from the Safe and mints fToken shares to the Safe. When depositing, the Fluid app asks only for the amount and sets no minimum shares, so the card asks only for the vault and the amount.AI research

Where deposits goAI research

Your USDC does not stay in fUSDC: in the same transaction it goes to Fluid's Liquidity contract, which holds the deposits of every Fluid market and lends them to Fluid's borrowing vaults and DEX pools. fUSDC only keeps account of your share. source 1 source 2

Getting the USDC backAI research

A large withdrawal can be delayed: Liquidity lets fUSDC take out only part of its deposits at a time, a limit that grows back over time, and only what borrowers have not borrowed. A small withdrawal under the base limit is not throttled. source 1 source 2

Who can change itAI research

Liquidity's code and its admins are changed by Instadapp governance through a timelock with a 1-day delay. The admins it names can change the withdrawal limits and the interest rates at once, and its guardians can pause deposits and withdrawals of a token at once. source 1 source 2 source 3

Next steps

  1. Before you approve, open each address's sources in Addresses and check that each domain is the protocol's own and that the page lists the address.
  2. The first time you use this template, or after it changes, approve it
    pnpm kiss-sign template approve fluid/vault-deposit

    It prints the template, the commit of main on GitHub and the template's keccak256. Answer y only if it is the one you reviewed.

  3. Make the card
    pnpm kiss-sign card new fluid/vault-deposit

    When templates/ or registry/ in your clone differs from main on GitHub, it stops before asking anything and tells you to run git pull first.

  4. vault: it lists the contracts you can pick, each with its full address. Pick fluid-fusdc, address 0x9Fb7b4477576Fe5B32be4C1843aFB1e55F251B33
